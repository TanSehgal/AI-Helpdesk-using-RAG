GREENFIELD INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY (GIT) - FEES (ADMINISTRATION)
NOTE: Greenfield Institute of Technology is a FICTIONAL university created as sample data
for this project. It does not represent any real institution.

=== FEE STRUCTURE ===
Tuition and other fees are charged per semester for all undergraduate and postgraduate
programs:

B.Tech Programs (CSE, ECE, ME, CE, AIDS):
- Tuition Fee: Rs. 85,000 per semester
- Development Fee: Rs. 8,000 per semester
- Examination Fee: Rs. 2,500 per semester
- Library and Lab Fee: Rs. 5,000 per semester
- Total per semester: Rs. 100,500

M.Tech Programs:
- Tuition Fee: Rs. 65,000 per semester
- Development Fee: Rs. 6,000 per semester
- Examination Fee: Rs. 2,000 per semester
- Total per semester: Rs. 73,000

MBA Program:
- Tuition Fee: Rs. 95,000 per semester
- Development Fee: Rs. 8,000 per semester
- Total per semester: Rs. 103,000

One-time fees (at admission only): Admission Fee Rs. 15,000, Caution Deposit
(refundable) Rs. 5,000.

Hostel and mess fees are billed separately (see Hostel department information).

=== FEE PAYMENT PROCEDURE ===
Fees can be paid through:
1. Online payment via the Student Information Portal (SIP) using net banking, UPI, or
   card (recommended, instant receipt)
2. Demand Draft in favor of "Greenfield Institute of Technology" payable at the
   registered city, submitted to the Accounts Office
3. Direct bank transfer to the university's designated account (details available on
   the SIP under "Fee Payment" > "Bank Transfer")

Fee payment deadlines:
- Odd Semester: On or before 20th July
- Even Semester: On or before 20th December

=== LATE PAYMENT AND FINES ===
A late fee of Rs. 100 per day applies after the deadline, up to a maximum of 30 days.
Beyond 30 days of non-payment, the student's registration may be canceled, subject to
review by the Dean of Student Affairs.

=== FEE REFUND POLICY ===
Students who withdraw from the program are eligible for a partial refund of tuition
fees based on the following schedule (as per applicable regulatory guidelines):
- Withdrawal before start of semester: 90% refund of tuition fee
- Withdrawal within 15 days of semester start: 50% refund of tuition fee
- Withdrawal after 15 days: No refund of tuition fee
Development, examination, and library fees are non-refundable in all cases. The
caution deposit is refunded in full upon graduation or withdrawal, provided there are
no outstanding dues.

=== INSTALLMENT FACILITY ===
Students facing financial difficulty may apply for a two-installment payment plan
through the Accounts Office, with 60% payable by the standard deadline and the
remaining 40% payable within 45 days, subject to approval by the Dean of Student
Affairs.

=== FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ===
Q: What is the total semester fee for a B.Tech student?
A: Rs. 100,500 per semester, covering tuition, development, examination, and library/
   lab fees. Hostel and mess fees are additional and billed separately.

Q: Can I pay my fees in installments?
A: Yes, with prior approval from the Dean of Student Affairs, you may pay 60% by the
   deadline and the remaining 40% within 45 days.

Q: I paid my fee but it's not reflecting on the portal. What should I do?
A: Contact the Accounts Office with your payment receipt/transaction ID; portal
   updates for bank transfers can take up to 3 working days.
