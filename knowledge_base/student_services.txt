GREENFIELD INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY (GIT) - GENERAL STUDENT SERVICES
NOTE: Greenfield Institute of Technology is a FICTIONAL university created as sample data
for this project. It does not represent any real institution.

=== STUDENT ID CARDS ===
Every enrolled student is issued a photo identity card during the admission process.
The ID card is mandatory for entry into campus, hostel, library, examination halls,
and labs.

Issuance procedure for new students:
1. Complete admission formalities and fee payment
2. Submit a passport-size photograph and a copy of government ID at the Student
   Services Desk (Admin Block, Room 110)
3. ID cards are issued within 5 working days of admission

Lost/Damaged ID Card:
1. Report the loss immediately to Student Services and file a request for reissue on
   the Student Information Portal
2. Pay the reissue fee of Rs. 250
3. Duplicate ID card is issued within 5 working days

=== COUNSELING AND WELLNESS SERVICES ===
GIT provides free and confidential counseling services through the Student Wellness
Center located in the Student Activity Center. Services include academic stress
counseling, career counseling, and mental health support. Appointments can be booked
through the Student Information Portal under "Wellness" or by walking in during
office hours (10 AM - 4 PM, Monday to Friday).

=== STUDENT CLUBS AND ACTIVITIES ===
GIT has over 20 registered student clubs covering technical, cultural, and sports
interests, including the Coding Club, Robotics Club, Music Society, Dramatics Club,
and the Sports Committee. New students can join clubs during the "Club Fair" held in
the first two weeks of the odd semester, coordinated through the Student Activity
Center.

=== GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL ===
General student grievances not related to academics or hostel (e.g., harassment,
facility issues, general complaints) can be raised through:
1. The Student Grievance Portal (accessible via the Student Information Portal)
2. In writing to the Dean of Student Affairs Office (Admin Block, Room 112)
Grievances are acknowledged within 48 hours and typically resolved within 2 weeks.

=== LOST AND FOUND ===
The Lost and Found desk is located at the Student Services Desk (Admin Block, Room
110). Items are logged and held for 60 days before being donated or disposed of.

=== FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ===
Q: How do I get a duplicate student ID card?
A: Report the loss to Student Services, submit a reissue request on the portal, and
   pay the Rs. 250 fee. The duplicate card is issued within 5 working days.

Q: Are counseling services confidential?
A: Yes, all sessions at the Student Wellness Center are confidential and free of
   charge for enrolled students.

Q: How do I join a student club?
A: Attend the Club Fair held in the first two weeks of the odd semester, or contact
   the Student Activity Center directly for ongoing enrollment in specific clubs.
