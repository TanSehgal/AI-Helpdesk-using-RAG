GREENFIELD INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY (GIT) - SCHOLARSHIPS (ADMINISTRATION)
NOTE: Greenfield Institute of Technology is a FICTIONAL university created as sample data
for this project. It does not represent any real institution.

=== SCHOLARSHIP CATEGORIES ===
GIT offers the following scholarship programs:

1. Merit Scholarship
   Awarded to students scoring a CGPA of 9.0 or above in the previous academic year.
   Covers 50% of tuition fee for the following year. Renewable each year subject to
   maintaining the CGPA requirement.

2. Need-Based Scholarship
   Awarded to students from economically weaker sections (family annual income below
   Rs. 3,00,000), based on submitted income certificates. Covers 25%-75% of tuition
   fee depending on assessed need, as determined by the Scholarship Committee.

3. Sports Scholarship
   Awarded to students who have represented their state or national team in
   recognized sporting events. Covers 25% of tuition fee. Applicants must submit
   proof of participation/achievement from the relevant sports authority.

4. GIT Alumni Scholarship
   Sponsored by the GIT Alumni Association for students with outstanding
   extracurricular contributions. Provides a fixed grant of Rs. 20,000 per year.

=== ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA ===
General eligibility for all scholarships:
- Must be a regular, full-time student of GIT
- Must not have any pending disciplinary action
- Must maintain minimum 75% attendance
- Cannot hold more than one GIT scholarship simultaneously (students are awarded the
  scholarship with the highest value if eligible for multiple)

=== APPLICATION PROCEDURE ===
Scholarship applications open twice a year, at the start of each semester, and must be
submitted through the Student Information Portal under "Scholarships" > "Apply".

Required documents:
1. Scholarship application form (auto-generated on the portal)
2. Previous semester mark sheet / CGPA statement
3. Income certificate (for need-based scholarship), issued by a competent government
   authority, not older than 6 months
4. Category certificate, if applicable
5. Supporting documents specific to the scholarship (sports certificates, achievement
   letters, etc.)

The Scholarship Committee reviews applications within 4 weeks and results are
published on the Student Information Portal. Scholarship amounts are credited
directly against the tuition fee for the relevant semester; they are not paid out as
cash to students.

=== EXTERNAL SCHOLARSHIPS ===
GIT also facilitates applications for government and external scholarships (such as
state government minority scholarships and national merit scholarships). Information
and application windows for these are circulated via official email and posted on the
notice board outside the Accounts Office.

=== RENEWAL AND CANCELLATION ===
Merit and need-based scholarships are reviewed every semester. A scholarship may be
canceled if:
- The student's CGPA falls below the required threshold
- The student is found guilty of academic dishonesty or disciplinary violation
- The student's attendance falls below 75% without valid condonation

=== FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ===
Q: Can I apply for both Merit and Need-Based scholarships?
A: You can apply for both, but if eligible for multiple, you will be awarded only the
   one with the highest value, not both simultaneously.

Q: What is the income limit for the Need-Based Scholarship?
A: Family annual income must be below Rs. 3,00,000, verified through a valid income
   certificate issued by a competent government authority.

Q: When do scholarship results get published?
A: Within 4 weeks of the application deadline, on the Student Information Portal
   under "Scholarships" > "Status".
