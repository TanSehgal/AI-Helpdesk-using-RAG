GREENFIELD INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY (GIT) - EXAMINATIONS DEPARTMENT
NOTE: Greenfield Institute of Technology is a FICTIONAL university created as sample data
for this project. It does not represent any real institution.

=== EVALUATION SCHEME ===
Each course is evaluated using a combination of continuous assessment and end-semester
examination:
- Internal Assessment (Continuous Evaluation): 40% weight
  - 2 Class Tests: 20%
  - Assignments/Quizzes: 10%
  - Attendance and Class Participation: 10%
- End-Semester Examination: 60% weight

Practical/lab courses follow a different scheme:
- Lab Records and Continuous Evaluation: 50%
- End-Semester Lab Examination: 50%

=== EXAM SCHEDULE ===
End-semester examinations are held twice a year:
- Odd Semester exams: First two weeks of December
- Even Semester exams: First two weeks of May

The detailed datesheet is published on the Examination Section notice board and the
university website at least 3 weeks before the examination period begins. Students can
also download their personalized exam hall ticket (admit card) from the Student
Information Portal 5 days before exams begin.

=== EXAM ELIGIBILITY ===
Students must have a minimum of 75% attendance (or approved condonation down to 65%)
and no pending dues to be eligible for the end-semester examination. Students with
pending library books, hostel dues, or fee dues will not receive their hall ticket
until the dues are cleared.

=== REVALUATION AND RECHECKING ===
Students who wish to have their answer scripts rechecked may apply within 7 days of
result declaration through the online portal. Rechecking fee is Rs. 300 per subject.
Rechecking involves verification of totaling and ensuring no question is left
unevaluated; it does not involve re-evaluation of the answers themselves.

=== SUPPLEMENTARY / BACKLOG EXAMINATIONS ===
Students who fail a course may appear for the supplementary examination conducted
approximately 6 weeks after the regular result declaration. Supplementary exam
registration must be completed within 10 days of result declaration, with a fee of
Rs. 400 per subject.

=== GRADING SYSTEM ===
GIT follows a 10-point grading scale:
O (Outstanding): 90-100 = 10 grade points
A+ (Excellent): 80-89 = 9 grade points
A (Very Good): 70-79 = 8 grade points
B+ (Good): 60-69 = 7 grade points
B (Above Average): 50-59 = 6 grade points
C (Average): 45-49 = 5 grade points
P (Pass): 40-44 = 4 grade points
F (Fail): Below 40 = 0 grade points

CGPA is calculated as the credit-weighted average of grade points across all completed
semesters.

=== RESULT DECLARATION ===
Results are typically declared within 3 weeks of the last examination date and are
published on the Student Information Portal. Physical mark sheets can be collected
from the Examination Section after 2 weeks of the online declaration.

=== FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ===
Q: I lost my hall ticket. What should I do?
A: Visit the Examination Section with your student ID card to get a duplicate hall
   ticket at least 2 days before your exam.

Q: Can I get my exam re-evaluated, not just rechecked?
A: No, GIT policy allows only rechecking (verification of totaling), not full
   re-evaluation of answers.

Q: When will I know if I have a backlog?
A: Backlogs are indicated in your semester result on the Student Information Portal
   with the grade "F" for the relevant course.
