GREENFIELD INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY (GIT) - ACADEMICS DEPARTMENT
NOTE: Greenfield Institute of Technology is a FICTIONAL university created as sample data
for this project. It does not represent any real institution.

=== ACADEMIC CALENDAR ===
GIT follows a semester system with two semesters per academic year:
- Odd Semester (Semester 1, 3, 5, 7): August to December
- Even Semester (Semester 2, 4, 6, 8): January to May
Each semester consists of 16 weeks of instruction followed by a 2-week examination period.

=== PROGRAMS OFFERED ===
GIT offers the following undergraduate programs, each 4 years (8 semesters) in duration:
- B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
- B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)
- B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering (ME)
- B.Tech in Civil Engineering (CE)
- B.Tech in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science (AIDS)

Postgraduate programs (2 years, 4 semesters):
- M.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering
- M.Tech in VLSI Design
- MBA in Technology Management

=== COURSE REGISTRATION ===
Course registration for each semester opens two weeks before the semester begins and
closes one week after classes start. Students register through the Student Information
Portal (SIP) using their university credentials. Late registration is allowed only with
written approval from the Head of Department (HoD) and carries a late fee of Rs. 500.

Each student must register for a minimum of 18 credits and a maximum of 24 credits per
semester. Students on academic probation are limited to a maximum of 16 credits.

=== ATTENDANCE POLICY ===
A minimum of 75% attendance is mandatory in each course to be eligible to sit for the
end-semester examination. Attendance is calculated from the first day of the semester.
Students with attendance between 65% and 75% may apply for condonation through the
Attendance Condonation Committee, subject to valid medical or personal grounds supported
by documentary evidence. Students below 65% attendance are automatically detained and
must repeat the course in a subsequent semester.

Medical leave: Students must submit a medical certificate within 7 days of returning to
classes for the absence to be considered for condonation.

=== SYLLABUS AND CURRICULUM ===
The detailed syllabus for each course is published on the department website at the
start of every semester and is also available at the Academic Section office (Admin
Block, Room 104). Each course syllabus includes: course objectives, unit-wise topics,
recommended textbooks, evaluation scheme, and course outcomes.

Curriculum revisions occur once every 4 years through the Board of Studies (BoS). The
current curriculum in effect is version 2023 (AC-2023).

=== ACADEMIC RULES ===
- Minimum CGPA of 5.0 is required to be promoted to the next academic year.
- Students with a CGPA below 5.0 for two consecutive semesters are placed on academic
  probation and counseled by their faculty advisor.
- A student may carry forward a maximum of 3 backlog (failed) courses to the next
  semester while continuing with the regular curriculum.
- Grade grievances must be submitted within 10 working days of result declaration
  through the online grievance portal.
- Academic dishonesty (plagiarism, copying, impersonation) in any assessment results in
  a zero for that assessment on the first offense and possible suspension on repeat
  offenses, as decided by the Academic Discipline Committee.

=== FACULTY ADVISORS ===
Each student is assigned a faculty advisor at the time of admission who provides
guidance on course selection, academic performance, and career planning. Faculty
advisor details are available on the Student Information Portal under "My Advisor".

=== FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ===
Q: How do I check my current attendance percentage?
A: Log in to the Student Information Portal (SIP) and navigate to "Attendance" under
   the Academics tab. Attendance is updated weekly by faculty.

Q: Can I change my elective course after registration?
A: Yes, but only during the "Add/Drop" period, which is open for the first 10 days of
   the semester. After this period, elective changes require HoD approval.

Q: What happens if I miss the registration deadline?
A: You must contact your faculty advisor and submit a late registration request with
   the Rs. 500 late fee through the Accounts Office.
