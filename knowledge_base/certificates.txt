GREENFIELD INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY (GIT) - CERTIFICATES & DOCUMENTS (ADMINISTRATION)
NOTE: Greenfield Institute of Technology is a FICTIONAL university created as sample data
for this project. It does not represent any real institution.

=== TYPES OF CERTIFICATES ISSUED ===
The Administration Office issues the following certificates and documents to
currently enrolled and former students:
1. Bonafide Certificate - confirms current enrollment status
2. Transfer Certificate (TC) - issued upon leaving the institution
3. Migration Certificate - required for students moving to another university
4. Character Certificate - confirms conduct record
5. Provisional Degree Certificate - issued immediately after final results, pending
   the formal convocation
6. Original Degree Certificate - issued after convocation
7. Consolidated Marksheet - a single document listing grades across all semesters
8. Fee Receipt / No Dues Certificate

=== APPLICATION PROCEDURE ===
Certificates can be requested through the Student Information Portal under
"Certificates" > "Apply". Steps:
1. Select the certificate type required
2. Specify the purpose (e.g., higher studies, employment, visa application)
3. Pay the applicable processing fee online
4. Track the request status via the portal ticket number

Certificates are typically ready for collection or digital download within the
following timelines:
- Bonafide Certificate: 2 working days
- Character Certificate: 3 working days
- Transfer Certificate / Migration Certificate: 7 working days (requires No Dues
  Clearance first)
- Provisional Degree Certificate: 15 working days after final result declaration
- Consolidated Marksheet: 5 working days
- Original Degree Certificate: Issued only after the annual convocation ceremony

=== PROCESSING FEES ===
- Bonafide Certificate: Free (up to 2 per semester), Rs. 100 for additional copies
- Character Certificate: Rs. 100
- Transfer Certificate: Rs. 500
- Migration Certificate: Rs. 500
- Duplicate Marksheet/Certificate (in case of loss): Rs. 1,000 plus a notarized
  affidavit of loss

=== NO DUES CLEARANCE ===
Before a Transfer Certificate, Migration Certificate, or final Provisional Degree can
be issued, students must obtain a "No Dues" clearance confirming no pending library
books, hostel dues, fee dues, or lab equipment. The No Dues form must be signed by:
Library, Hostel Office (if applicable), Accounts Office, and the respective
Department Office. The completed form is submitted to the Administration Office.

=== DIGITAL CERTIFICATES ===
Bonafide certificates and consolidated marksheets are also available as digitally
signed PDF documents downloadable directly from the Student Information Portal,
without needing to visit the Administration Office, for most standard purposes.

=== FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ===
Q: How long does it take to get a Bonafide Certificate?
A: Typically 2 working days after applying on the Student Information Portal; it can
   also be downloaded digitally in most cases.

Q: Do I need No Dues Clearance for a Bonafide Certificate?
A: No, No Dues Clearance is only required for Transfer Certificate, Migration
   Certificate, and final Provisional Degree Certificate.

Q: I lost my original marksheet. How do I get a duplicate?
A: Submit a request on the portal along with a notarized affidavit of loss and pay
   the Rs. 1,000 processing fee. Processing takes approximately 10 working days.
