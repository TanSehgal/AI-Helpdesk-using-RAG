GREENFIELD INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY (GIT) - HOSTEL DEPARTMENT
NOTE: Greenfield Institute of Technology is a FICTIONAL university created as sample data
for this project. It does not represent any real institution.

=== HOSTEL FACILITIES ===
GIT has 4 hostels on campus:
- Hostel A (Boys, First Year) - 200 rooms, capacity 400
- Hostel B (Boys, Senior) - 250 rooms, capacity 500
- Hostel C (Girls, First Year) - 150 rooms, capacity 300
- Hostel D (Girls, Senior) - 180 rooms, capacity 360

Each room is either a double-sharing or triple-sharing room, equipped with beds,
study tables, chairs, cupboards, Wi-Fi connectivity, and 24-hour water and electricity
supply with backup power.

=== HOSTEL APPLICATION PROCEDURE ===
Hostel accommodation is offered on a first-come-first-served basis, subject to
availability, and is mandatory for all first-year outstation students.

To apply for hostel admission, students must submit the following documents to the
Hostel Office (located near Hostel A reception) within 10 days of admission:
1. Duly filled Hostel Application Form (available on the Student Information Portal
   under "Hostel" > "Apply")
2. Two recent passport-size photographs
3. Copy of the student ID card (or admission confirmation letter if ID card is not
   yet issued)
4. Copy of Aadhaar card or other government-issued photo ID
5. Medical fitness certificate from a registered medical practitioner
6. Hostel fee payment receipt (see Hostel Fees section below)

Applications are reviewed by the Hostel Warden's office, and room allocation is
communicated via email and the Student Information Portal within 5 working days.

=== HOSTEL ALLOCATION ===
Room allocation is done by the Hostel Committee based on: year of study, application
date, and any medical or special accommodation requests. First-year students are
allotted rooms in Hostel A or Hostel C. Senior students may apply for room changes at
the start of each academic year through the "Room Change Request" form, subject to
availability.

Roommate preferences can be indicated in the application form but are not guaranteed.

=== HOSTEL FEES ===
Hostel fees are charged per semester and include accommodation and maintenance
(mess charges are billed separately, see below):
- Double-sharing room: Rs. 45,000 per semester
- Triple-sharing room: Rs. 35,000 per semester
- Refundable security deposit (one-time, at first admission): Rs. 10,000

Hostel fees must be paid in full before check-in, or in two installments with prior
approval from the Hostel Warden (50% before check-in, 50% within 6 weeks of semester
start). Late payment attracts a fine of Rs. 100 per day.

=== MESS FACILITIES ===
Each hostel has an attached mess providing breakfast, lunch, evening snacks, and
dinner. Mess timings:
- Breakfast: 7:30 AM - 9:30 AM
- Lunch: 12:30 PM - 2:30 PM
- Evening Snacks: 5:00 PM - 6:00 PM
- Dinner: 8:00 PM - 10:00 PM

Mess fee is Rs. 18,000 per semester, billed separately from hostel accommodation fees
and payable at the start of each semester. A vegetarian-only mess option is available
in Hostel C and Hostel D. Students with specific dietary requirements can submit a
request to the Mess Committee.

=== HOSTEL RULES ===
- Hostel gates close at 10:30 PM on weekdays and 11:30 PM on weekends. Late entry
  requires prior written permission from the Warden.
- Visitors are allowed in the common visiting area only, between 4:00 PM and 7:00 PM.
  Overnight guests are not permitted.
- Ragging of any kind is strictly prohibited and will result in immediate expulsion
  from the hostel, in line with UGC anti-ragging regulations.
- Cooking appliances (induction stoves, heaters) are not allowed in rooms due to fire
  safety regulations.
- Students must vacate hostel rooms within 3 days of the end of the semester unless
  prior permission is obtained for vacation stay.

=== HOSTEL COMPLAINTS ===
Complaints regarding room maintenance, mess quality, or hostel facilities can be
raised through:
1. The "Hostel Complaints" section of the Student Information Portal (tracked with a
   ticket number)
2. In person at the Hostel Warden's office (available 10 AM - 1 PM, Monday to Friday)
3. The Hostel Committee's suggestion box located at each hostel reception

Complaints are typically resolved within 3-5 working days. Urgent issues (electricity,
water supply, safety) are treated as priority and addressed within 24 hours.

=== FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ===
Q: What documents are required for hostel admission?
A: A duly filled hostel application form, two passport-size photographs, a copy of the
   student ID card (or admission confirmation letter), a government photo ID, a medical
   fitness certificate, and the hostel fee payment receipt.

Q: Can day scholars apply for hostel accommodation?
A: Yes, day scholars may apply if seats are available after outstation students are
   accommodated, subject to Hostel Committee approval.

Q: Is the security deposit refundable?
A: Yes, the Rs. 10,000 security deposit is refunded within 30 days of final hostel
   checkout, after deduction of any damage charges.
