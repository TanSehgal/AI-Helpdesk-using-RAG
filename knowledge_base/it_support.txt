GREENFIELD INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY (GIT) - IT SUPPORT (STUDENT SERVICES)
NOTE: Greenfield Institute of Technology is a FICTIONAL university created as sample data
for this project. It does not represent any real institution.

=== IT SERVICES OVERVIEW ===
The Campus IT Helpdesk supports all student-facing digital services, including the
Student Information Portal (SIP), campus Wi-Fi, university email accounts, and
computer lab systems.

=== STUDENT INFORMATION PORTAL (SIP) ACCOUNT ===
Every student receives an SIP account and a university email address
(firstname.lastname@student.greenfieldinstitute.edu.example) upon admission.
Login credentials are sent to the student's personal email during the admission
process.

Password reset: Students can reset their SIP password using the "Forgot Password"
link on the login page, which sends a reset link to the registered personal email.
If the personal email is no longer accessible, students must visit the IT Helpdesk
in person with their student ID card for manual verification and reset.

=== CAMPUS WI-FI ===
Wi-Fi is available across all academic buildings, hostels, and common areas under the
network name "GIT-Campus". Students connect using their SIP credentials. Each student
account supports up to 3 simultaneous device connections. Wi-Fi issues (slow speed,
connectivity drops) can be reported to the IT Helpdesk through the "IT Support"
ticket system on the portal.

=== COMPUTER LABS ===
GIT has 6 general-purpose computer labs and department-specific labs (CSE, ECE, AIDS)
available for student use outside class hours, from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM on working
days, subject to availability and lab-in-charge approval for after-hours access.

=== EMAIL AND SOFTWARE ===
University email accounts come with access to Microsoft Office 365 Education
(including Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Teams) free of charge for enrolled students.
Students can also request access to specialized software (MATLAB, AutoCAD, etc.)
through the IT Helpdesk, subject to department-specific licensing availability.

=== RAISING AN IT SUPPORT TICKET ===
Students can raise an IT support ticket through:
1. The Student Information Portal under "IT Support" > "Raise Ticket"
2. Emailing the helpdesk directly
3. Walking in to the IT Helpdesk (Academic Block, Room 015)

Ticket priority levels:
- Critical (portal/email down for many users): Response within 2 hours
- High (individual account access issues): Response within 1 working day
- Normal (software requests, general queries): Response within 3 working days

=== DATA AND ACCOUNT SECURITY ===
Students are advised to never share their SIP password with anyone, including staff.
The IT Helpdesk will never ask for a password over email or phone. Suspicious emails
requesting credentials should be reported to the IT Helpdesk immediately as a
potential phishing attempt.

=== FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ===
Q: I forgot my SIP portal password. How do I reset it?
A: Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page to receive a reset link at your
   registered personal email. If that email is inaccessible, visit the IT Helpdesk
   with your student ID card for manual reset.

Q: How many devices can I connect to campus Wi-Fi?
A: Up to 3 simultaneous devices per student account on the "GIT-Campus" network.

Q: Is Microsoft Office free for students?
A: Yes, Microsoft Office 365 Education is provided free of charge with your
   university email account.
