GREENFIELD INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY (GIT) - LIBRARY (STUDENT SERVICES)
NOTE: Greenfield Institute of Technology is a FICTIONAL university created as sample data
for this project. It does not represent any real institution.

=== LIBRARY OVERVIEW ===
The Dr. A.K. Rao Central Library is located in the Academic Block and holds
approximately 85,000 physical books, 12,000 e-books, and subscriptions to over 150
international journals across engineering, science, and management disciplines.

=== LIBRARY TIMINGS ===
- Monday to Friday: 8:00 AM - 10:00 PM
- Saturday: 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM
- Sunday: 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM (Reading Room only, no book issue/return)
- During examination periods, the library remains open until midnight on weekdays.

=== BOOK BORROWING POLICY ===
- Undergraduate students: Up to 4 books for 14 days
- Postgraduate students: Up to 6 books for 21 days
- Faculty members: Up to 10 books for 30 days

Books can be renewed twice online through the Library Portal if not reserved by
another user. Reference books, journals, and rare collections are for in-library use
only and cannot be borrowed.

=== FINES AND PENALTIES ===
- Late return fine: Rs. 2 per day per book
- Lost book: Replacement cost of the book plus a processing fee of Rs. 200
- Damaged book: Assessed by the librarian, ranging from partial to full replacement
  cost depending on the extent of damage

Students with unpaid library fines exceeding Rs. 500 will have their portal account
and hall ticket issuance suspended until dues are cleared.

=== DIGITAL RESOURCES ===
The library provides access to digital resources including IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect,
SpringerLink, and the National Digital Library, accessible on-campus and remotely via
VPN using the student's university credentials. E-books and past year question papers
can be downloaded from the Library Portal.

=== LIBRARY CARD AND ACCESS ===
The student ID card also functions as the library card. New students receive library
access automatically after fee payment and ID card issuance. Lost ID cards must be
reported immediately to both Student Services and the Library to block borrowing
privileges (see Student Services documentation for ID card reissue).

=== STUDY SPACES ===
The library has a Reading Room (250 seats), a Silent Study Zone, Group Discussion
Rooms (bookable via the Library Portal, up to 2 hours per booking), and a Digital
Resource Center with 40 computer workstations.

=== FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ===
Q: How many books can an undergraduate student borrow at once?
A: Up to 4 books for a period of 14 days, renewable twice if not reserved by another
   student.

Q: What is the fine for a late return?
A: Rs. 2 per day per book. Fines exceeding Rs. 500 will suspend portal and hall
   ticket access until cleared.

Q: Can I access journals from home?
A: Yes, digital resources like IEEE Xplore and ScienceDirect are accessible remotely
   via VPN using your university login credentials.
