GREENFIELD INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY (GIT) - TRANSPORTATION (STUDENT SERVICES)
NOTE: Greenfield Institute of Technology is a FICTIONAL university created as sample data
for this project. It does not represent any real institution.

=== BUS SERVICE OVERVIEW ===
GIT operates a fleet of 25 buses covering 18 routes across the city for day-scholar
students and staff. Buses run on a fixed schedule, departing from designated pickup
points in the morning and returning after college hours.

=== ROUTES AND TIMINGS ===
Bus routes are grouped into 4 zones (North, South, East, West) covering major
residential areas. Full route maps and stop-wise timings are published on the
Transportation Portal (accessible via the Student Information Portal under
"Transport") at the start of each academic year.

General schedule:
- Morning pickup: 6:45 AM - 8:15 AM (routes depart in a staggered manner)
- Evening drop: 4:30 PM and 6:30 PM (two drop trips to accommodate students with
  extra classes or lab sessions)

=== BUS PASS APPLICATION ===
Students wishing to use the bus service must apply for a bus pass through the
Transportation Portal:
1. Select the nearest pickup point/route
2. Pay the semester bus fee (see Fees section below)
3. Bus pass is issued digitally and linked to the student ID card within 3 working
   days

=== TRANSPORTATION FEES ===
Bus fees are charged per semester based on distance zone:
- Zone 1 (0-10 km): Rs. 6,000 per semester
- Zone 2 (10-20 km): Rs. 9,000 per semester
- Zone 3 (20+ km): Rs. 12,000 per semester

Fees are payable along with the semester fee deadline. Mid-semester joiners are
charged on a pro-rata basis.

=== ROUTE CHANGES AND NEW STOPS ===
Requests for new stops or route changes can be submitted to the Transportation Office
through the portal and are reviewed quarterly by the Transportation Committee,
subject to a minimum number of students (10+) requesting the same stop.

=== RULES ===
- Bus passes are non-transferable; the ID card is checked at boarding.
- Students must be present at the pickup point at least 5 minutes before the
  scheduled time; buses do not wait beyond 2 minutes past schedule.
- Misconduct on buses (damage to property, misbehavior with driver/conductor) may
  result in suspension of the bus pass, decided by the Transportation Committee.

=== FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ===
Q: How do I apply for a bus pass?
A: Apply through the Transportation Portal, select your nearest pickup point, and pay
   the applicable zone fee. The pass is linked to your ID card within 3 working days.

Q: What happens if I miss the bus?
A: Buses do not wait beyond 2 minutes past the scheduled time. Students who miss the
   bus must arrange alternate transportation; no refund is provided for missed trips.

Q: Can I request a new bus stop near my home?
A: Yes, submit a request via the Transportation Portal. New stops are considered
   quarterly and require at least 10 students requesting the same location.
